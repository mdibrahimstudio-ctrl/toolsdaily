Text to Speech

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box (up to 10,000 characters). The word count and estimated reading time update as you type.
  2. Pick a voice from the dropdown — the voices come from your device and browser. Adjust the speed, pitch and volume sliders to taste.
  3. Press "Speak" and listen. Pause and Resume work mid-speech, and Stop ends it. The line currently being read is shown below the buttons.

FAQ

Which voices can I choose from?

The voices come from your own device and browser — Windows, macOS, Android and ChromeOS each ship different voices, and Chrome adds its own Google voices. Pick any voice listed in the dropdown; the list is filled in automatically when your browser reports its voices.

Can I listen to long articles or documents?

Yes, up to 10,000 characters at a time. Long text is read in short chunks, sentence by sentence, so browsers that cut off very long utterances keep reading smoothly to the end.

Why does the speech pause sometimes?

Some browsers pause speech synthesis when the tab is in the background or after a long stretch. Press Resume to continue — the reader picks up from the current chunk. Keeping the tab visible and the volume up gives the smoothest playback.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The text is read aloud by your browser's built-in speech engine on your own device. Nothing is uploaded or saved anywhere.
