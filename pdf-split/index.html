PDF Split

Pull just the pages you need out of a long PDF — a chapter, a form, a receipt.

📂 Drop a PDF here, or

Download extracted PDF

How to use

  1. Choose a PDF — the tool shows how many pages it has.
  2. Type the pages to extract, for example 1-3 or 2,5.
  3. Click “Extract pages” and download the new PDF with just those pages.

FAQ

How do I write the page range?

Use commas and dashes: “1-3” means pages 1 to 3, “2,5” means pages 2 and 5, and you can mix them like “1-3, 7”. Pages are saved in their original order.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The split happens entirely in your browser — the file never leaves your device.

Why did my file fail to load?

Password-protected (encrypted) or damaged PDFs cannot be read. Remove the password or repair the file and try again.
