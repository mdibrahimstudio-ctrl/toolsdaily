How to use

  1. Drop a PDF onto the box (or click to choose one).
  2. Pick a text watermark — or switch to the Image tab and upload your logo — then set the size, opacity, angle and pages.
  3. Click “Add watermark”, then download your watermarked PDF.

FAQ

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Watermarking happens entirely in your browser — your PDF and your logo never leave your device.

Can someone remove the watermark afterwards?

The watermark is baked into each page like printed ink, so it shows in every PDF reader — but a determined person with a PDF editor can still remove it. It deters casual copying; it is not security. For real protection, set a password with Protect PDF.

Can I watermark only some pages?

Yes. Leave the Pages box empty to watermark every page, or enter specific pages — for example “1,3-5” stamps pages 1, 3, 4 and 5 only.
