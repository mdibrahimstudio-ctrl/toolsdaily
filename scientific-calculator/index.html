Scientific Calculator

A full scientific calculator with trig, logs, powers, roots and factorial — plus a history of your last calculations.

History

    How to use

    1. Tap the keypad — or type in the display — to build your expression, using brackets to group parts of it.
    2. Press = to calculate. The result appears in the display and the calculation is added to your history below.
    3. Use Ans to reuse your last answer, the copy button to copy the result, and Clear to start again.

    FAQ

    How do I use the scientific calculator?

    Tap the keypad buttons — or type directly in the display — to build an expression, then press = to calculate it. Use the function keys for square root, powers, trigonometry and logarithms, and use Ans to reuse your last answer in the next calculation.

    Are the trig functions in degrees or radians?

    Degrees. sin(30) gives 0.5, cos(60) gives 0.5 and tan(45) gives 1, because the angles are read as degrees, which is what most school and everyday calculations expect.

    Is my calculation data sent anywhere?

    No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser — your expressions and results never leave your device, and nothing is stored on a server.
