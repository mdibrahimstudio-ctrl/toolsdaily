FAQ

What is ROI?

ROI (return on investment) measures how much profit an investment made relative to what it cost. It is calculated as (total return - investment) / investment x 100. A 25% ROI means you earned $25 for every $100 you invested.

How is annualized ROI (CAGR) calculated?

Annualized ROI, also called CAGR (compound annual growth rate), spreads the total return evenly across the years: ((total return / investment) ^ (1 / years) - 1) x 100. It shows the steady yearly growth rate that would turn your investment into your total return.
