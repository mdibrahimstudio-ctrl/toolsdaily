FAQ

How is GPA calculated?

Each grade is worth points on a 4.0 scale (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0), with plus and minus grades worth a little more or less. Your GPA is the total grade points (grade points times credit hours for each course) divided by your total credit hours.

What is a good GPA?

On the 4.0 scale, a GPA of 3.5 or higher is generally considered very good, 3.0 to 3.49 is good, and 2.0 is usually the minimum to stay in good academic standing. What counts as good also depends on your school and your goals.

How does the target GPA helper work?

Tell it your current GPA, how many credits you have finished, your target GPA and how many credits you plan to take. It works out the average grade you need in those planned credits — and tells you plainly if the target is out of reach.
