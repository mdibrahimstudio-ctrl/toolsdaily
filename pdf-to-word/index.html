PDF to Word

Convert a PDF into an editable Word (.docx) file you can keep working on. Text, paragraphs and headings are preserved — but complex layouts, tables and images are simplified, so this is not a pixel-perfect conversion. Your file never leaves your device.

How to use

  1. Click the box (or drop a file onto it) and choose one PDF.
  2. Click “Convert to Word” — text, paragraphs and headings are carried over into an editable document.
  3. Click “Download .docx” to save your Word file.

FAQ

Is the conversion pixel-perfect?

No. Text, paragraphs and headings are preserved, but complex layouts, tables and images are simplified — this is not a pixel-perfect conversion. The output is an editable Word document, not a visual replica of the PDF.

What if my PDF is a scan with no text?

Scanned or image-only PDFs contain no extractable text, so there is nothing to convert into Word. Use the OCR PDF tool instead — it reads the text out of scanned pages first.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser — your files never leave your device.
