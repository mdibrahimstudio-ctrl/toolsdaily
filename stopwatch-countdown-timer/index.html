    How to use

    1. Stopwatch: press "Start", then "Lap" to record split times — the fastest and slowest laps are marked. "Pause" freezes the time, "Reset" clears it.
    2. Countdown: type hours, minutes and seconds, or pick a preset like 25 minutes for a Pomodoro session, then press "Start". The bar shows how much time is left.
    3. When the countdown reaches zero you will see "Time's up!" and hear three beeps (keep the tab open and unmuted). Switch between the two timers with the tabs at the top.

    FAQ

    How accurate is the stopwatch?

    The stopwatch measures time from your device's own clock, so it stays accurate to the centisecond even if the browser tab is in the background or your computer is busy. The display updates roughly 30 times a second.

    Can I use the countdown timer for Pomodoro sessions?

    Yes. The 25-minute preset is the classic Pomodoro work session, and the 5-minute preset works as the short break. Set any hours, minutes and seconds you like, or pick a preset and press Start.

    Does the timer keep running if I switch tabs?

    Yes. Both timers are driven by timestamps, not by counting ticks, so switching tabs or minimising the browser does not lose time. Keep the tab open and unmuted to hear the countdown alarm when it finishes.

    Is my data sent anywhere?

    No. The stopwatch and countdown timer run entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved anywhere.
