FAQ

What is a fancy text generator?

It turns plain text into stylish Unicode characters that look like different fonts — bold, italic, script, bubble letters and more — so your Instagram bio, TikTok caption or gaming name stands out.

Will fancy text work on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp?

Yes. The styles are ordinary Unicode characters, not a font, so they paste and display anywhere that accepts text — Instagram bios and captions, TikTok, WhatsApp, Discord, X and most games.

Are these real fonts?

No — they are special Unicode characters (mathematical bold, script, circled and fullwidth letters) that look like different fonts. That is why they work in bios and captions where you cannot change the font.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. All 18 styles are generated entirely in your browser — your text is never uploaded or saved anywhere.
