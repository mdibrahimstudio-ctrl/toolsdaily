    How to use

    1. Pick a die type and how many dice to roll, then click “Roll the dice” — each die shows separately with the total, and every roll joins the history.
    2. Flip the coin whenever you need a quick yes-or-no decision; heads and tails are counted for you.
    3. Clear the history any time to start a fresh game.

    FAQ

    Are the rolls truly random?

    Yes. Every roll uses your browser's built-in cryptographic random generator, the same kind used for security features — not a predictable pattern.

    What do d4, d6 and d20 mean?

    The number after the d is how many sides the die has: d4 has 4 sides (1 to 4), d6 is a normal six-sided die, and d20 has 20 sides. Tabletop games like Dungeons and Dragons use the whole set.

    How many dice can I roll at once?

    Up to 10 dice in one roll. Each die result is shown separately, with the total underneath, and every roll is added to the history below.

    Does the coin flip keep score?

    Yes. Every flip is counted, so you can see your running totals of heads and tails at a glance.
