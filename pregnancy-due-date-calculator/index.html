How to use

  1. Enter the first day of your last menstrual period — the first day of bleeding, not spotting.
  2. Set your average cycle length (usually 28 days). If your cycles are shorter or longer, adjust it between 21 and 35 days.
  3. Click “Calculate due date” to see your estimated due date, how many weeks pregnant you are and your trimester dates. Copy or download the summary to keep it.

FAQ

How is the due date calculated?

Your estimated due date is the first day of your last menstrual period plus 280 days — 40 weeks.

How accurate is the due date?

It is an estimate — only about 1 in 20 babies arrive exactly on the due date. Your doctor's ultrasound dating is more accurate.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — the calculation happens entirely in your browser.
