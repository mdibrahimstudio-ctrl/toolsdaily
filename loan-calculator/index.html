FAQ

How is a loan payment calculated?

With the standard amortization formula: payment = P x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments. Each payment first covers that month's interest, and the rest reduces the loan balance.

Will paying extra each month really save me money?

Yes. Every extra dollar goes straight to the principal, so the balance falls faster and less interest builds up in later months. For example, an extra $100 a month on a $100,000 loan at 6% over 30 years pays the loan off about 9 years sooner and saves roughly $40,000 in interest.

Is my loan information sent anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser — your loan amount, rate and term never leave your device and nothing is stored or sent to a server.
