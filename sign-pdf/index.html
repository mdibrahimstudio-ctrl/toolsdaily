How to use

  1. Drop a PDF onto the box (or click to choose one).
  2. Create your signature — draw it on the canvas, type your name, or upload a signature image.
  3. Choose the pages, position and size, click “Sign PDF”, then download your signed file.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded to a server when I sign it?

No. Signing happens entirely in your browser — your PDF and your signature never leave your device.

Does this add a legally binding digital signature?

No. It stamps a visible image of your signature onto the page, like signing on paper. It does not add a cryptographic digital signature or certificate.

Which pages will my signature appear on?

You choose: all pages, the last page only, or specific pages (for example “1,3”). The signature is placed at the bottom of each chosen page, in the position and size you pick.
