How to use

  1. Enter the original price and the discount percentage — tap a quick discount button or type your own.
  2. Set the quantity if you are buying more than one item.
  3. Click “Calculate” to see the final price, your total savings, the original total and the price per item after discount. Copy the summary or download it as a .txt file.

FAQ

How is the discount calculated?

The discount is taken off the original price: discount amount = original price × discount % ÷ 100, and final price = original price − discount amount. For example, 25% off $120 is a $30.00 discount, so you pay $90.00. The quantity multiplies both the original total and the final total.

Does the discount calculator include tax?

No. The calculator works on the price before tax, so tax, shipping and any extra fees are not included. Add tax to the final price afterwards if you need the checkout total.

Can I use it for multiple items?

Yes. Set the quantity to the number of items you are buying. The calculator shows the original total, the total savings and the final total for all items, plus the discounted price of a single item.
