FAQ

What is PMI and when do I pay it?

PMI (private mortgage insurance) protects the lender if you stop paying your mortgage. Most lenders charge it when your down payment is under 20% of the home price. This calculator adds PMI automatically when your down payment is below 20% and drops it at 20% or more.

How is the monthly mortgage payment calculated?

Your principal and interest payment comes from the standard amortisation formula, using your loan amount (home price minus down payment), your annual interest rate and your loan term. Property tax, home insurance and PMI, if any, are added on top to give your total monthly payment.

Is my information sent anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser — your home price and loan details never leave your device and nothing is stored or sent to a server.
