Age Calculator

Your exact age — down to the day — plus some fun totals.

–Age
–Total days
–Total weeks
–Days to next birthday

How to use

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Optionally choose a different “age at” date — leave it empty to use today.
  3. Click “Calculate age” to see your exact age, total days and weeks lived, and the countdown to your next birthday.

FAQ

How exact is this calculator?

It counts real calendar days, including leap years, so totals are exact for the dates you enter.

Can I calculate age on a future date?

Yes — set the “age at” date to any future date, for example a school or job application deadline.

Is my birth date stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is saved.
