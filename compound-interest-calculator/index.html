How to use

  1. Enter your initial deposit, how much you will add each month, the annual interest rate and how many years you will save for.
  2. Pick how often the interest compounds — monthly compounding grows a little faster than annual compounding at the same rate.
  3. Your future value, total contributions and interest earned update instantly. Download the full year-by-year schedule as a .csv file.

FAQ

How is compound interest calculated?

Your balance earns interest each compounding period, then that interest is added to the balance so the next period earns interest on a larger amount. Contributions are added at the end of each compounding period. Over time the growth speeds up, because you keep earning interest on your earlier interest.

What is the difference between compound and simple interest?

With simple interest you only earn interest on your original deposit, so the amount you earn each year stays the same. With compound interest you earn interest on your deposit plus all the interest you have already earned, so the amount you earn each year grows over time.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser — your amounts never leave your device and nothing is stored or sent to a server.
