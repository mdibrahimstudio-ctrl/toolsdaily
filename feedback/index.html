FAQ

Do you reply to every message?

We read every message. If you leave your email and your message needs an answer, we reply to it — usually within a few days.

What happens to my email address?

It is only used to reply to you. We never share it or use it for anything else.

Can I suggest a tool that does not exist yet?

Yes — that is exactly what “Suggest a new tool” is for. Tell us what the tool should do and we will consider it for a future release.
