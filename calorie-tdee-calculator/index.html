How to use

  1. Enter your age and sex, then your weight and height — in metric or imperial units.
  2. Pick the activity level that best matches a typical week for you.
  3. Click “Calculate” to see your BMR and TDEE, plus daily calorie targets for cutting, maintaining or bulking. The results update live as you change any value.

FAQ

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body burns at rest to keep you alive — breathing, circulation and cell repair. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) is your BMR multiplied by an activity factor, so it estimates the total calories you burn in a typical day including movement and exercise.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

A common starting point is to eat about 500 calories below your TDEE each day, which is shown as the cut figure above. Eating at your TDEE maintains your weight, and eating about 500 calories above it supports gaining weight. These are estimates — talk to a doctor or dietitian for personal advice.

Is my data stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser — nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
