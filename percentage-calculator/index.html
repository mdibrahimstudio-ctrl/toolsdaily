FAQ

How do I find a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 200 x 15 / 100 = 30.

How is percentage change calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. A rise from 80 to 100 is (100 - 80) / 80 x 100 = +25%.

Is anything I type stored?

No. All three calculators run in your browser — your numbers are never saved or sent anywhere.
