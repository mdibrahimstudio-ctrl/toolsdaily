How to use

  1. Enter your first fraction — type a fraction like 1/2, a mixed number like 1 1/2, or a whole number like 5.
  2. Pick an operation, enter your second fraction, then click “Calculate” — the answer updates as you type too.
  3. See the simplified answer, its decimal value and the step-by-step working. Copy the result to share or save it.

FAQ

How do I enter a mixed number like 1 1/2?

Type the whole number, a space, then the fraction — for example, type 1 1/2 for one and a half. You can also type a plain fraction such as 3/4 or a whole number such as 5. The calculator converts mixed numbers to improper fractions before doing the maths, so 1 1/2 becomes 3/2.

How are the answers simplified?

Every answer is reduced to its lowest terms by dividing the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor (GCD). For example, 12/18 simplifies to 2/3 because both numbers divide by 6. Whole-number answers are shown as a single number, such as 2 instead of 2/1.

What happens if I divide by zero?

Dividing by zero is not allowed, so the calculator shows an error instead of an answer. That includes a fraction with a zero denominator, such as 1/0, and dividing by a fraction that equals zero, such as 1/2 divided by 0. Check your inputs and try again with a non-zero denominator.
