FAQ

How does text become binary?

Every character is first turned into its UTF-8 bytes, then each byte is written as 8 binary digits (bits). The letter A, for example, is one byte: 01000001.

Can I convert emoji and accented letters?

Yes. The converter uses UTF-8, so accented letters, non-Latin scripts and emoji all convert correctly — they just take more than one byte each, so their binary is longer.

Why is my binary invalid?

Binary input must contain only the digits 0 and 1 (spaces and line breaks are fine), and the total number of bits must be a multiple of 8. Anything else — letters, other digits, or a half-finished byte — is rejected with an error.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser — your text never leaves your device.
