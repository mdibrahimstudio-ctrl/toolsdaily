FAQ

What is the largest number Roman numerals can show?

Roman numerals in the standard system go up to 3,999 (MMMCMXCIX). There is no symbol for zero, and numbers above 3,999 need extra notation such as a bar over a numeral, which this converter does not use — so it accepts numbers from 1 to 3,999 only.

Why is 4 written as IV and not IIII?

Roman numerals use subtractive notation: a smaller numeral placed before a larger one is subtracted. So 4 is IV (5 minus 1), 9 is IX, 40 is XL, 90 is XC, 400 is CD and 900 is CM. Writing IIII or VV breaks the rules, so this converter rejects those forms as invalid.

How do I convert a Roman numeral back to a number?

Type the numeral in the Roman numeral box — for example, MCMXCIV — and click Convert to Number. The converter checks every character and the order of the symbols, then shows the number (1994 for MCMXCIV). Lowercase letters work too, so xiv converts to 14.
