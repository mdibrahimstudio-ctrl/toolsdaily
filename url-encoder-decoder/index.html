How to use

  1. Type or paste text or a URL, then choose whether to encode it as a component or as a full URL.
  2. Press Encode to percent-encode it, or Decode to turn an encoded URL back into readable text. Tick "Decode + as a space" for form data, where + means a space.
  3. Copy the result or download it as a .txt file.

FAQ

What is URL encoding?

URL encoding (percent-encoding) replaces characters that are not safe in a web address with a % sign followed by two hexadecimal digits — for example a space becomes %20 and é becomes %C3%A9. It lets text with spaces, symbols or non-English letters travel safely inside a URL.

When should I encode a full URL instead of a component?

Encode a full URL when the whole address must stay usable — it keeps : / ? & and = untouched and only encodes the unsafe parts, like spaces. Encode a component when a piece of text goes inside a URL, for example a search term in a query string — then even / ? & and = are encoded so they cannot break the address apart.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Encoding and decoding happen entirely in your browser — your text is never uploaded or saved anywhere.
