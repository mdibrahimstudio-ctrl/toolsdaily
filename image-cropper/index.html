How to use

  1. Choose any image from your device, or drop it onto the box above.
  2. Drag the white box to choose what stays, pull a corner handle to resize it, pick an aspect ratio preset, or type exact pixel coordinates.
  3. Pick the output format, click “Crop image” and download the result.

FAQ

Will cropping reduce my image quality?

No. The cropped area is saved at the original resolution, pixel for pixel — cropping only removes the parts outside the box, it never re-scales or blurs what stays.

What aspect ratios can I crop to?

Free (any shape), square 1:1, landscape 4:3, 3:2 and 16:9, and portrait 3:4, 2:3 and 9:16. Pick a preset and the crop box locks to that shape while you drag it.

Can I crop to an exact pixel size?

Yes. Type the exact X, Y, width and height in pixels under the preview — the crop box jumps to those coordinates, and the downloaded image is exactly that size.

Are my images uploaded?

Never. The cropping happens entirely in your browser.
