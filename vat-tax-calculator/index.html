How to use

  1. Enter the amount, then pick a tax rate — tap a common-rate button or type your own.
  2. Choose “Add tax” if your amount is the price before tax, or “Remove tax” if it already includes tax.
  3. Click “Calculate” to see the net, tax and gross amounts. Copy the summary or download it as a .txt file.

FAQ

Add or remove tax — which mode do I pick?

Pick 'Add tax' when the amount you entered is the price before tax (the net price) and you want the tax added on top — for example, a $100 net price at 20% becomes $120 gross. Pick 'Remove tax' when the amount you entered already includes tax (the gross price) and you want to work out how much of it is tax — for example, a $100 gross price at 20% contains $16.67 of tax and a net price of $83.33.

How is the tax extracted from a gross price?

The net price is the gross price divided by 1 plus the tax rate — at 20%, divide the gross price by 1.20. The tax is then the gross price minus the net price. For a $100 gross price at 20%, that gives a net price of $83.33 and tax of $16.67. The calculator does the maths in whole cents, so half a cent always rounds up.

What VAT rates are used around the world?

Rates vary a lot by country. The UK charges a standard VAT rate of 20%, most EU countries charge between 15% and 27%, and the US has no federal VAT — sales tax there varies by state and even by city, from 0% in some states to over 10% combined in others. Use the preset buttons for common rates, or type any rate you need.
