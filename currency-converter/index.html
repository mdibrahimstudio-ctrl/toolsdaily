  1. Enter an amount and pick the From and To currencies — the result updates live as you type.

  3. Live rates load automatically; if you are offline the page falls back to the bundled rates snapshot dated 2026-11-17 and says so on the page.

FAQ

Where do the live exchange rates come from, and how often are they updated?

Live rates come from open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API), a free public exchange-rate service, and are refreshed when the page loads and whenever you change the From currency. The provider updates its rates daily. Amounts are shown with 2 decimal places, except Japanese yen (JPY) and South Korean won (KRW), which are shown with no decimal places.

What happens if I am offline?

If the live rates cannot be fetched, the converter falls back to a bundled snapshot of rates as of 2026-11-17 and tells you so on the page. Conversions still work, but the rates may be slightly out of date — reconnect and reload the page for the latest live rates.

Are my amounts uploaded anywhere?

No. Your amounts are converted in your browser and are never uploaded or stored. The only network request is the public exchange-rate lookup, which sends no personal data and no amounts — only the currency code.
