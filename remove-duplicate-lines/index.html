How to use

  1. Paste your text — one item per line — into the input box.
  2. Choose your options: ignore case to treat "Apple" and "apple" as the same line, trim spaces, drop empty lines, or sort the result alphabetically.
  3. Press "Remove duplicates". The first occurrence of each line is kept and later copies are dropped. Copy the result or download it as a .txt file.

FAQ

What does a duplicate line remover do?

It keeps the first occurrence of each line and deletes every later copy, so a list with repeated entries becomes a clean list of unique lines — handy for cleaning up email lists, keyword lists, log files and pasted data.

Does it keep the original order of my lines?

Yes — by default your lines stay in the order they first appear. Tick "Sort lines alphabetically" if you would rather have the result in A–Z order instead.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Removing duplicates happens entirely in your browser — your text is never uploaded or saved anywhere.
