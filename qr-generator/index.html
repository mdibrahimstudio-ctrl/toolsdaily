QR Code Generator

Turn any text or link into a scannable QR code, then download it as a PNG image.

How to use

  1. Type or paste any text, link, Wi-Fi name or message into the box.
  2. Your QR code appears instantly as you type.
  3. Pick a PNG size and click “Download PNG” to save it for printing or sharing.

FAQ

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The QR code is generated entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

What size PNG should I download?

512 × 512 is fine for screens and small prints. Choose 1024 × 1024 for posters or large prints.

Why did I get an error?

QR codes have a capacity limit. Very long text (several thousand characters) cannot fit — shorten it and try again.
