How to use

  3. Click “Generate password”, check the strength label, then copy it into your password manager.

FAQ

Are these passwords really random?

Yes. They are generated with your browser's cryptographic random source (crypto.getRandomValues), never with predictable Math.random().

Is my password sent anywhere?

No. It is created on your device and only copied when you click Copy.

How long should a password be?

Length matters most. A 16+ character password with mixed types is strong enough for almost any account.
