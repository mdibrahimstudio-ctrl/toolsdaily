FAQ

How much should I tip at a restaurant?

In the United States, 15–20% of the pre-tax bill is the usual range for good table service — 15% for standard service and 20% (or more) for great service. In many other countries tipping is smaller or already included as a service charge, so check the bill first.

How do I split the bill between friends?

Enter the bill amount and your tip percentage, then set the number of people. The calculator shows each person's share of the total bill and of the tip, so everyone pays the same fair amount.

Is my bill amount sent anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser — your bill amount never leaves your device and nothing is stored or sent to a server.
