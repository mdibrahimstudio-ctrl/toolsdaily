How to use

  1. Click the box (or drop a file onto it) and choose one PDF.
  2. Pick the number format, position, font size — and the page numbering starts at (use 2 to skip a cover page).
  3. Click “Add page numbers”, then download your numbered PDF.

FAQ

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Numbering happens entirely in your browser — your documents never leave your device.

Can I skip numbering the cover page?

Yes. Set the start page to 2 and the first page stays unnumbered. The number always matches the page's real position in the document, so the second page of a two-page PDF reads “Page 2 of 2”.

Does numbering change the rest of my PDF?

No. Your pages, text and images stay exactly as they are — only a small number is added near the edge of each page, in the format, size and position you choose.
