Number to Words Converter

Turn any number into English words — plain words, cheque style, or currency amounts.

–Words

How to use

  1. Type a number — for example 1234 — into the number field.
  2. Pick a style: plain words, cheque style, or currency.
  3. Click Convert to see the result in words, or copy it straight to your clipboard.

FAQ

How do I convert a number to words?

Type the number into the field and pick a style — plain words, cheque style, or currency — then click Convert. The result appears instantly, and you can copy it with one click. The converter works for whole numbers, decimals, and negative numbers.

What is the largest number this converter can handle?

The converter handles numbers up to 999 trillion, which is 999,999,999,999,999. Anything larger returns an error, because the words get too long to be useful. Zero, negative numbers, and decimals are all supported within that range.

Can I use the result on a cheque or in a document?

Yes. Cheque style capitalises the first letter and adds the word only at the end, for example One thousand two hundred thirty-four only. Currency mode writes the amount in dollars and cents, which is handy for invoices, contracts, and other documents. Always double-check the amount before you use it anywhere official.
