How to use

  1. Drop an image onto the box, or click to choose a JPG, PNG or WebP file.
  2. Move the quality slider — 80% is a good balance of size and looks. Optionally set a maximum width to resize big photos.
  3. Click “Compress image”, compare the sizes, then download your smaller file.

FAQ

Does compression reduce quality?

JPEG compression trades a little quality for a much smaller file. At 80% most people cannot see any difference.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything happens on your device using your browser's built-in image tools.

Which format is the output?

The compressed file is a JPEG, the most widely supported format for photos.
