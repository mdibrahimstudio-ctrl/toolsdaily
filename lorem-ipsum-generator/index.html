FAQ

What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is placeholder text used by designers and developers to fill a layout before the real words are ready. It looks like Latin, but it is mostly scrambled pseudo-Latin, so readers focus on the design instead of the words.

Is the text different every time?

Yes. Each click generates a fresh mix of classic lorem ipsum words, unless you tick 'Start with the classic sentence', which always opens with 'Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet…'.

Can I use the generated text anywhere?

Yes. Lorem ipsum is in the public domain — use it in mockups, wireframes, templates and demos without credit or restriction.
