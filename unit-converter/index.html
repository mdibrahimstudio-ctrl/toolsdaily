Convert length, weight and temperature units instantly.

How to use

  1. Pick a category: length, weight or temperature.
  2. Enter a value and choose the “from” and “to” units — the result updates instantly.
  3. Use the swap button to reverse the conversion.

FAQ

How accurate are the conversions?

They use the official international definitions (for example 1 inch = exactly 25.4 mm), so results are as accurate as your input.

Which units are included?

Length: millimetres to miles. Weight: grams to tonnes, including pounds and ounces. Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

Can I convert currency here?

Not yet — exchange rates change constantly, so a currency tool needs live data, unlike these fixed unit conversions.
