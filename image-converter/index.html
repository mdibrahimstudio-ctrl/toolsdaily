FAQ

PNG, JPEG or WebP — which should I choose?

JPEG is best for photos, PNG keeps transparency and sharp edges, and WebP usually gives the smallest files for websites.

Will I lose transparency?

JPEG does not support transparency, so transparent areas become white. PNG and WebP keep transparency.

Are my images uploaded?

Never. The conversion happens entirely in your browser.
