How to use

  1. Enter your pay amount and pick the period it covers — hourly, weekly, biweekly, monthly or annual.
  2. Set your regular hours per week, and tick the overtime box if you work extra hours at time-and-a-half.
  3. Click “Calculate” to see the equivalent pay for every period. Copy the summary or download it as a .txt file.

FAQ

How is annual pay calculated?

Annual pay is your hourly rate multiplied by your hours per week and then by 52 weeks — so $25 an hour at 40 hours a week is $25 × 40 × 52 = $52,000 a year. Monthly pay is the annual figure divided by 12, biweekly pay is the annual figure divided by 26, and weekly pay is the annual figure divided by 52.

How does overtime work in this calculator?

Tick the overtime box and enter your overtime hours per week. Overtime is paid at time-and-a-half — 1.5 times your normal hourly rate — for those hours, on top of your regular pay. For example, at $20 an hour with 10 overtime hours a week, overtime adds $300 a week, so weekly pay is $1,100.

Does this calculator include tax?

No — all figures are gross pay, before tax and other deductions. Your take-home pay will be lower once income tax, social security and any other deductions are taken out.
