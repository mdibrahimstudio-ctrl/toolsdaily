FAQ

How many square feet are in one decimal?

1 decimal = 435.6 square feet; 1 acre = 100 decimals

Why does katha have different values in different places?

this converter uses the Bangladesh standard — 1 katha = 720 square feet and 1 bigha = 20 katha. In parts of India the katha is different, so always confirm the local standard before a land deal

Is my data sent anywhere?

No — the conversion happens entirely in your browser.
