    FAQ

    Which image formats can I upload?

    Any image your browser can open: PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF and BMP. Screenshots and photos of QR codes work too, as long as the code is sharp and fully visible.

    Does my image leave my device?

    No. Decoding happens entirely in your browser with a library bundled on this page. Your image is never uploaded to a server.

    Why does it say no QR code was found?

    The code may be blurry, cropped, too small, or photographed at a steep angle. Try a sharper, straight-on photo with the whole code — including its quiet white border — in frame.
