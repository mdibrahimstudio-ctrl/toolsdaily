How to use

  1. Drop a PDF onto the box, or click it to choose one file.
  2. Pick a quality — High keeps the most detail, Medium balances size and quality, Low makes the smallest file.
  3. Click “Compress PDF”, compare the before and after sizes, then download the smaller file.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser — your document never leaves your device.

Why is the text in my compressed PDF no longer selectable?

Each page is re-rendered as an image and rebuilt into a new PDF, which is what makes the file smaller. The text still looks the same, but it can no longer be selected or searched.

What if the compressed PDF is not smaller?

Some PDFs are already tightly compressed, so re-rendering them may not save space. The tool always shows the before and after sizes honestly — if there is no saving, you can still download the result or try the Low quality setting.
