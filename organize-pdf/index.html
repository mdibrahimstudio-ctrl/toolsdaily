Organize PDF

Delete, reorder and rotate the pages of a PDF in one place — fix a scanned document or tidy a file before you share it.

Drop one PDF file here, or

Download organized PDF

How to use

  1. Click the box (or drop a file onto it) and choose one PDF file.
  2. Under each page thumbnail, use the arrow buttons to move it, Rotate to turn it 90°, or Delete to remove it. Reset restores the original document.
  3. Click “Save organized PDF”, then download your reorganized file.

FAQ

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Organizing happens entirely in your browser — your documents never leave your device.

Can I rotate a page more than once?

Yes. Each click on Rotate turns the page 90° clockwise, so four clicks bring it back to where it started. The rotation is saved into the downloaded PDF.

What if I delete every page by mistake?

You cannot — the tool always keeps at least one page. If you try to delete the last remaining page it will tell you, and the Reset button restores the original order, rotation and deleted pages.
