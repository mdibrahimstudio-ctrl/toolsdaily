How to use

  1. Enter the minimum and maximum of your range, and how many numbers you need.
  2. Tick “Unique numbers only” for a draw with no repeats, and “Sort results” to list them smallest first.
  3. Press Generate, then copy the numbers or download them as a .txt file.

FAQ

Is the random number generator truly random?

Yes. It uses your browser's built-in cryptographic random generator (crypto.getRandomValues), the same kind of randomness used for secure passwords — not a predictable pattern. No numbers are sent to a server; everything happens on your device.

How do I get unique numbers with no repeats?

Tick 'Unique numbers only' before pressing Generate. The tool will only return numbers that have not appeared before. Note the count cannot be larger than the size of your range — for example, you cannot draw 10 unique numbers from 1–5.

What can I use a random number generator for?

Raffles and prize draws, picking a winner, rolling dice, choosing a random sample, classroom or game use, and any time you need a fair, unbiased pick.
