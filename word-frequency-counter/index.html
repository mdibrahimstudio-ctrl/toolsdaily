How to use

  1. Paste or type your text into the box — the sample text is already there, so replace it with your own.
  2. Choose whether to ignore case and common stop words, and how many top words to show. The counts update live as you type.
  3. Copy the ranked list or download it as a .txt file to use in your writing, SEO checks or research.

FAQ

What does the word frequency counter count?

It counts every word in your text and shows how many times each word appears, plus its share of all the words. Words are sequences of letters and digits, so don't counts as one word and 123 counts as a word too.

What are stop words, and should I ignore them?

Stop words are very common words like the, and, of and to that appear in almost any text. Turn on Ignore common stop words to hide them from the results, so the words that actually carry meaning in your text rise to the top.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. The counting happens entirely in your browser — your text never leaves your device and nothing is stored or sent to a server.
