How to use

  1. Press Start, then click in the typing box and type the words shown above it.
  2. Keep typing for 60 seconds — your live WPM and accuracy update as you type.
  3. When the time runs out, check your results, copy them if you like, or press Restart for a fresh set of words.

FAQ

How is WPM calculated in a typing test?

WPM stands for words per minute. One word counts as five characters, so your WPM is your correct characters divided by five, divided by the minutes you typed. For example, 250 correct characters in 60 seconds is 50 WPM. Only correctly typed characters count toward your WPM.

How long is the typing test?

The test lasts 60 seconds. The timer starts when you press Start or type your first character, and it stops automatically when the time runs out. You can press Restart at any time to get a fresh set of words and try again.

Is my typing saved or uploaded anywhere?

No. The test runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you type is uploaded or stored. Your best WPM is kept only in memory while this page stays open, and it disappears when you close or reload the page.
