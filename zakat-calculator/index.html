Zakat Calculator

Add up your zakatable wealth, check it against the nisab, and see exactly how much zakat you owe — 2.5% on what is above the threshold.

Zakat is 2.5% of your net zakatable wealth when it reaches the nisab. The nisab is the lower of 87.48 grams of gold and 612.36 grams of silver, valued at the prices you enter. Net zakatable wealth = cash + gold value + silver value + business assets − debts.

Gold and silver prices change daily — update the price fields to current market rates for an accurate result.

    How to use

    1. Enter your cash, business assets and stock, plus the weight of any gold and silver you own — and the current price per gram for each metal.
    2. Subtract anything you owe in the debts field. Your net zakatable wealth is checked against the nisab automatically — the lower of the gold and silver nisab values.
    3. If your wealth is at or above the nisab, your zakat is 2.5% of it. Copy the itemized summary or download it as a .txt file.

    FAQ

    What is nisab?

    The minimum amount of wealth a Muslim must own before zakat becomes due — set by the value of 87.48 grams of gold or 612.36 grams of silver.

    How much zakat do I pay?

    2.5% of your total zakatable wealth above the nisab, after subtracting debts.

    Is my data sent anywhere?

    No — the calculation happens entirely in your browser.
