How to use

  1. Drop an audio file onto the box, or click to choose an MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, WebM or FLAC file.
  2. Pick the spoken language, or leave it on Auto detect, then click “Transcribe”. The first time, the AI model downloads once (about 42 MB) — after that it starts right away.
  3. Read the transcript, copy it, or download it as a .txt file — or as an .srt subtitle file with timestamps.

FAQ

Is my audio uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely on your device, inside your browser. Your audio never leaves your phone or computer — the only thing downloaded is the AI model itself, once.

Why does the first use download about 42 MB?

That download is the AI speech model itself — OpenAI's Whisper, shrunk to fit in a browser. Because the AI lives on your device instead of a server, it needs to arrive once. Your browser remembers it, so later visits start instantly.

Which formats and languages are supported?

MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, WebM and FLAC audio files. Whisper understands around 100 languages — leave the language on Auto detect, or pick English, Bangla, Hindi, Urdu, Spanish, French, Arabic and more from the list.

How accurate is the transcription?

Clear speech in a quiet recording transcribes very well. Background noise, music, overlapping voices and heavy accents lower accuracy, so treat the result as a strong first draft and proofread it before you rely on it.

How long can my audio be?

There is no fixed limit, but the work happens on your device: a one-hour recording can take a few minutes on a fast computer and much longer on a phone. Keep this tab open while it works, and prefer shorter clips on older devices.

Is it really free?

Yes — no sign-up, no per-minute credits. The AI runs on your own device, so there is no server bill to pass on to you.
